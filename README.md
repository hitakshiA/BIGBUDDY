# BIG-BUDDY 🤝  
**Connect. Learn. Grow.**

## 📌 Overview  
BIG-BUDDY is a mentorship and guidance mobile application designed to bridge the gap between users and domain experts. It provides a holistic and accessible space for individuals to receive personalized support in areas such as mental health, career planning, and skill development through real-time interactions.

## 🚀 Features  
- 🎯 **Expert Connections** – Connect with counselors, professors, public speakers, and industry professionals  
- 🧠 **Holistic Guidance** – Support for mental well-being, career growth, and personal development  
- 💬 **Live Sessions** – One-on-one and group mentoring in real time  
- ⭐ **Ratings & Reviews** – User-driven feedback for transparency and trust  
- 🔍 **Personalized Experience** – Tailored guidance based on user needs  

## 🛠️ Problem Statement  
Access to reliable and personalized mentorship remains limited for many individuals. Existing platforms often lack real-time interaction, accessibility, and trust-driven feedback systems.

## 💡 Solution  
BIG-BUDDY solves this problem by offering a centralized mobile platform that connects users with verified experts. The app ensures quality interactions through live sessions and a user-driven review system, empowering users to confidently navigate personal and professional challenges.

## 🎯 Target Audience  
- Students seeking academic and career mentorship  
- Professionals aiming to upskill or transition careers  
- Individuals seeking mental health and personal development support  

## 🌟 Impact  
BIG-BUDDY promotes growth, confidence, and informed decision-making by making expert guidance easily accessible through a mobile-first experience.

## 🧩 Tech Stack  
- **Platform:** Android  
- **Language:** Java  
- **UI Design:** XML  
- **IDE:** Android Studio  

## 🏁 Future Scope  
- AI-based expert recommendations  
- Secure in-app video calling  
- Multilingual support  
- Community forums and peer interactions
