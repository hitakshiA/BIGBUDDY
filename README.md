# BIG-BUDDY 🤝  
**Connect. Learn. Grow.**

## 📌 Overview  
BIG-BUDDY is a mentorship and guidance platform designed to bridge the gap between users and domain experts. It offers a holistic, accessible space for individuals to receive personalized support in areas such as mental health, career planning, and skill development through real-time interactions.

## 🚀 Features  
- 🎯 **Expert Connections** – Connect with counselors, professors, public speakers, and industry professionals  
- 🧠 **Holistic Guidance** – Support for mental well-being, career growth, and personal development  
- 💬 **Live Sessions** – One-on-one and group mentoring in real time  
- ⭐ **Ratings & Reviews** – User-driven feedback for quality assurance  
- 🔍 **Personalized Experience** – Tailored guidance based on individual needs  

## 🛠️ Problem Statement  
Finding reliable, timely mentorship and professional guidance remains a challenge for many individuals. Existing solutions are often fragmented, inaccessible, or lack personalization.

## 💡 Solution  
BIG-BUDDY centralizes expert mentorship into a single platform, enabling users to connect with verified professionals in a trusted and interactive environment. Reviews and ratings ensure transparency and quality.

## 🎯 Target Audience  
- Students seeking academic and career mentorship  
- Working professionals aiming to upskill or transition careers  
- Individuals seeking mental health and personal development support  

## 🌟 Impact  
BIG-BUDDY empowers users to make informed decisions, build confidence, and grow personally and professionally by making expert guidance easily accessible.

- **Backend:** Node.js / Firebase
